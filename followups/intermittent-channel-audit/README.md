# Intermittent-channel audit

This folder publishes the completed artifact-only pilot of the frozen C4
residual-geometry projections.

It selected 43 of 1,024 probes on validation; 42/43 were positive on the
frozen test set and 31/43 remained above the preregistered 0.02 observed-effect
floor. The supported interpretation is temporally clustered activity with a
smaller order-dependent component across 32-token blocks. The pilot does not
support a claim of stable signed payload, semantic content, or causal use.

- [`REPORT.md`](REPORT.md) — results and limitations
- [`PROTOCOL.md`](PROTOCOL.md) — locked estimator and controls

The large frozen projection tensors and corpus data are intentionally omitted.
